# Brief: Monkyvision podcast-intro test

## The assignment (as received, 30 Oct 2026)

> You will receive a ZIP file containing the raw podcast footage, including separate video and audio
> clips of the host and the guest. Your task is to identify the most engaging, impactful, and
> attention-grabbing moments, and creatively edit them into a high-energy podcast intro of
> approximately 1 minute 20 seconds.

- **Deliverable:** a high-engagement intro of about 80 s, 16:9 for YouTube.
- **Craft:** motion graphics, dynamic cuts, creative transitions, engaging visual elements; fast-paced,
  visually appealing, professionally executed.
- **References** (tone, pacing, structure, audience fit): Raj Shamani, The Diary Of A CEO, Somrat Dutta.
- **Music:** if used, it must complement the pacing without overpowering the dialogue.
- **Submission:** within 2 days. The final export goes in the same folder the raw clip was supplied in.
- **Watermark:** allowed (the work is used only to evaluate the candidate).

## Reference study

The first 95 s of nine recent videos (three per channel) were cut-detected, contact-sheeted and checked
for audio hits. The takeaways that shaped this edit:

| Channel | What carries the intro | Used here as |
|---|---|---|
| **Raj Shamani** | Rapid cold open of the guest's sharpest lines, cut against host reactions. Small white sentence line plus one huge condensed keyword, italic-serif emphasis words, a big guest-name card, context labels | League Gothic keyword slams, Playfair italic emphasis, the SIMONE KNEGO name card, host laugh cutaways, mono context chips |
| **The Diary Of A CEO** | A story arc in miniature: hook, stakes, the vulnerable moment, the turn, a thesis line, then a clean show ident. The score drops away for the hard part | Hook → barrage → R.E.A.L. → descent (score dies, picture desaturates) → comeback → thesis → ident |
| **Somrat Dutta** | Full-screen white word cards, punchy punch-in jump cuts, word-timed text | The white **ACCOUNTANT.** card, 1.0/1.2/1.45× punch-ins on every cut |
